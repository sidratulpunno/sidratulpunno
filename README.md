## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hi! I'm **Sidratul Punno**, an IoT and Robotics Engineering student, researcher, and developer passionate about building intelligent, efficient, and impactful technology.

- 🎓 **Education:** B.Sc. in IoT & Robotics Engineering, University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh
- 📊 **Academic standing:** CGPA 3.87/4.00
- 🔬 **Research:** Machine Learning for IoT Security, Quantum-Inspired ML, Edge AI, and Assistive AI
- ⚡ **Engineering:** GPU-accelerated computing, embedded Linux, robotics, and production-ready Flutter applications
- 📚 **Publications:** Research published in IEEE conferences and the IJIEEB journal
- 🏆 **Achievements:** Dean's Award for three consecutive years and President's Scout Award
- 🌱 **Currently exploring:** LLM fine-tuning, LoRA, quantum-inspired learning, and Yocto-based embedded Linux

> My goal is to bridge research and real-world engineering — from efficient machine learning algorithms to intelligent systems running at the edge.
